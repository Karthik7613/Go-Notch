Privacy Policy

Data Collection
This app monitors WhatsApp messages from groups you are part of to detect trip-related keywords. Messages are stored locally on your device server only.

No Sharing
Your data is never shared with any third party. All processing happens on your own server.

WhatsApp Connection
This app uses the WhatsApp Web protocol. Your session credentials are stored locally and never transmitted to external servers.

Cookies & Storage
We use browser localStorage to store your saved trips and UI preferences only.
